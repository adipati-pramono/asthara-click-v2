Loaders

Eightcellent loop, linear, mark and inline trio. All gold, one per surface.

Eightcellent loop
2.4 s · ease-in-out · indeterminate
Signature loader. Eight bars travel in a wave — echoing the eight pillars and the Eightcellent ribbon. Full-page, drawer, modal.
Linear1.8 s
Top edge of a sheet, table, or panel. Becomes determinate when % is known.
Mark1.6 s · 2.6 s
Splash / app boot. Mark breathes while a thin gold arc orbits.
Inline trio1.2 s
Inside buttons, chips, and table cells mid-request. Sits on ink so gold reads.
UseGold (or gold-tint track). One loader per surface. Always paired with a status string after 400 ms.
AvoidSpinners that bounce, overshoot, or rotate full mark. No coloured loaders outside gold.
